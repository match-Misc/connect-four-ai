# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## NUC gets stuck on Minis Forum boot screen
**Problem:** The NUC powers on but gets stuck on the Minis Forum boot screen. There is no spinning loading wheel or any other sign of activity.
**Solution:** Wait for about 20 seconds. If nothing changes, press and hold the power button on the NUC until it completely shuts off. Then press the power button again to boot normally. This should resolve the issue.
